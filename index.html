Beyond Arithmetic: The Semantic Bottleneck in Neuro-Symbolic LLMs

Authors: Bogdan-Marius Dinu, Ciprian-Mihai Ceausescu, Cristian-Hacic Kevorchian

Accepted for publication and oral presentation at the 30th International Conference on Knowledge-Based and Intelligent Information & Engineering Systems (KES 2026), Dublin, September 2026. Read the paper (PDF)

  • Proposed a multi-agent distributed framework combining neuro-symbolic reasoning (DeepSeek-R1 8B + SymPy) with Semantic RAG to eliminate execution-level arithmetic hallucinations in edge-scale LLMs. Evaluated on benchmark subsets: GSM8K (21 problems), MATH500 (100), SVAMP (100), AIME 2025 (30).
  • Identified the Semantic Bottleneck: once arithmetic is made deterministic, natural language understanding becomes the limiting factor (92% on competition-level MATH500 against 64% on the linguistically adversarial SVAMP). On AIME 2025 the raw 8B baseline scored 0% and the 14B model 23.3%, while the same 8B model inside the pipeline reached 66.6%, showing that structural augmentation beats parameter scaling at edge scale.
